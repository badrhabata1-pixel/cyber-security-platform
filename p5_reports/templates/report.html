Definitions

Alert severityCritical: an attack likely succeeded. High: an active attack in progress. Medium: suspicious behaviour that needs review. Low: unusual but probably benign activity.
Domain risk score0-100, combining threat-intelligence feeds (VirusTotal, Google Safe Browsing), registration age, TLS certificate, SPF/DMARC e-mail protection and phishing URL patterns. 75+ critical, 50+ high, 25+ medium.
Failed loginsFailed password attempts and attempts against usernames that do not exist (SSH and platform logins).
Threat levelCritical when an unresolved critical alert or a critical-risk domain exists; High for unresolved high-severity activity; Elevated when only suspicious activity was seen.
